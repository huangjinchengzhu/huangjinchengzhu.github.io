邀月（MoonVite）隐私权政策

生效日期：2026年9月27日　|　最后更新：2026年9月27日

欢迎使用“邀月（MoonVite）”。本隐私权政策适用于由 Google Play 开发者名称：朕乃万岁 发布、 作者/开发者：张辉武 开发的“邀月（MoonVite）”应用。

我们重视您的隐私。本应用为单机/离线应用，主要用于帮助用户记录和管理月经周期等个人信息。 在您正常使用本应用的情况下，您输入的月经周期及相关记录不会被上传到开发者服务器， 我们也不会通过本应用建立用户账号或出售您的个人信息。

2. 我们处理哪些数据

“邀月（MoonVite）”设计为本地运行的单机应用。根据当前版本的产品设计， 应用不会主动将以下用户输入内容上传或传输给开发者：

  • 月经开始日期、结束日期及周期记录；
  • 经期、排卵期等由用户记录或应用根据本地数据计算的信息；
  • 用户自行填写的备注、体重、身材管理等记录（如当前版本提供相关功能）；
  • 欲望/生理周期相关记录（如当前版本提供相关功能）；
  • 用户为自己、女儿或其他对象建立的本地记录资料；
  • 应用根据上述本地数据生成的统计、预测或展示结果。
重要说明： 上述信息可能属于个人信息或健康相关敏感信息。它们仅用于应用在您的设备上提供相应功能。 开发者不会通过本应用主动收集、出售或向第三方提供这些信息。

3. 数据是否上传或与第三方共享

本应用的核心功能为离线运行。根据当前版本的设计，您的个人记录不会被上传至开发者服务器， 也不会为了广告、分析、营销或其他商业目的出售或共享给第三方。

本应用不要求用户注册账号，也不要求用户登录开发者服务器。

第三方 SDK 说明： 本隐私权政策基于当前版本不包含会收集、上传或共享用户个人/健康数据的第三方 SDK 的前提。 如果未来版本增加广告、统计分析、崩溃报告、云同步或其他第三方服务， 开发者将在相关版本更新隐私权政策，并按照 Google Play 要求更新 Data Safety（数据安全）申报。

4. 数据存储方式

您主动输入的记录主要存储在您的设备本地，并由应用用于提供周期记录、统计和相关功能。 开发者无法通过本应用直接查看您存储在设备上的这些记录。

如果您使用 Android 系统提供的设备备份、恢复或迁移功能， 数据是否被系统备份以及备份方式可能由 Android 系统、设备制造商和您的系统设置决定。 这类系统级备份不属于开发者主动上传行为。

5. 数据保留和删除

由于应用不在开发者服务器上建立用户账户或保存用户记录，开发者不存在需要在服务器端长期保留的用户记录。

您可以根据应用当前提供的删除功能删除本地记录；如果应用版本未提供单独的删除入口， 您也可以通过 Android 系统的应用设置清除应用数据，或卸载应用。 清除应用数据或卸载应用可能导致保存在设备本地的相关记录被删除。

如果未来版本增加账号、云同步或服务器存储功能，本政策将相应更新，并提供与该功能相匹配的数据删除方式。

6. 数据安全

我们采用与本应用离线运行方式相匹配的措施保护本地数据。 由于主要数据存储在您的设备上，设备本身的锁屏密码、系统安全设置、备份设置以及第三方设备管理方式 也会影响数据安全。建议您使用安全的设备锁屏方式，并妥善管理设备访问权限。

7. 权限

本应用仅在实现具体功能确有必要时请求 Android 系统权限。 如果某个版本新增需要访问照片、通知、日历、健康数据、设备存储或其他系统资源的功能， 应用将在相关功能中说明其用途，并按照适用的 Android 和 Google Play 政策处理。

8. 健康与医疗说明

“邀月（MoonVite）”属于经期记录和周期管理工具。应用中的周期计算、日期预测、统计或其他展示结果 仅用于个人记录和一般信息参考，不构成医疗诊断、治疗或专业医疗建议。

如您存在健康问题、异常出血、严重疼痛、月经周期明显异常或其他需要医疗判断的情况， 请咨询合格的医疗专业人员。

9. 儿童隐私

本应用不是专门面向儿童设计的服务。我们不会通过本应用主动建立儿童用户账号或收集儿童个人信息。 如果您为女儿或其他家庭成员建立本地记录，该记录按照本政策作为设备上的本地数据处理。

10. 隐私权政策的更新

当应用功能、数据处理方式或适用法律和平台政策发生变化时，我们可能更新本隐私权政策。 更新后的政策将在本页面发布，并更新“最后更新”日期。

Privacy Policy for MoonVite (邀月)

Effective Date: September 27, 2026 | Last Updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to MoonVite (邀月), published on Google Play under the Developer Name: 朕乃万岁-道法自然，顺其自然 and developed by Zhang Huiwu (张辉武).

We respect your privacy. MoonVite is designed as a standalone/offline application for recording and managing menstrual cycles and related personal information. During normal use, information entered into the app is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to a developer-operated server. The app does not require a user account or login to a developer server.

1. Developer and Privacy Contact

App Name: MoonVite (邀月)

Google Play Developer Name: 朕乃万岁

Developer/Author: Zhang Huiwu (张辉武)

Privacy Contact Email: 280880560@qq.com

For questions about this Privacy Policy, privacy practices, or personal data handling, please contact the developer using the email address above.

2. Data We Handle

MoonVite is designed to operate locally on your device. Based on the current version, the app does not actively upload the following information to the developer:

  • Menstrual start dates, end dates, and cycle records;
  • Period or ovulation information recorded or calculated locally by the app;
  • Notes, weight, body-management records, where such features are available;
  • Desire/libido or cycle-related records, where such features are available;
  • Local profiles created for yourself, your daughter, or another person;
  • Statistics, predictions, or results generated from the above local information.
Important: Some of the information listed above may constitute personal information or health-related sensitive information. It is used locally on your device to provide the app's features. The developer does not sell or disclose this information to third parties through the app.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

The core functionality of MoonVite is offline. Based on the current version, your personal records are not uploaded to a developer-operated server and are not sold or shared with third parties for advertising, analytics, marketing, or other commercial purposes.

The app does not require users to register an account or log in to a developer server.

Third-party SDKs: This Privacy Policy is based on the current version not including any third-party SDK that collects, uploads, or shares users' personal or health-related data. If a future version adds advertising, analytics, crash reporting, cloud synchronization, or another third-party service, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety disclosure will be updated accordingly.

4. Data Storage

Information that you enter into the app is primarily stored locally on your device and is used to provide cycle tracking, statistics, and related features. The developer cannot directly view these locally stored records through the app.

If you use Android's system backup, restore, or device-transfer features, whether and how app data is backed up may depend on Android, your device manufacturer, and your system settings. Such system-level backup is not an upload initiated by the developer.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because the app does not create user accounts or store user records on developer-operated servers, the developer does not retain user records on its servers.

You may delete local records using any deletion function provided by the current version of the app. If the current version does not provide a separate deletion function, you may clear the app's data through Android system settings or uninstall the app. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app may delete records stored locally on the device.

If a future version introduces accounts, cloud synchronization, or server-side storage, this Privacy Policy will be updated and an appropriate deletion mechanism will be provided.

6. Data Security

We use safeguards appropriate to the app's offline design to protect locally stored data. Because data is primarily stored on your device, your device passcode, system security settings, backup settings, and device-management practices also affect data security. We recommend using a secure device lock and protecting access to your device.

7. Permissions

The app requests Android permissions only when they are necessary for a particular feature. If a future version requests access to photos, notifications, calendars, health data, device storage, or other system resources, the relevant purpose will be explained and the feature will be handled in accordance with applicable Android and Google Play policies.

8. Health and Medical Disclaimer

MoonVite is a menstrual tracking and cycle management tool. Cycle calculations, predictions, statistics, and other information provided by the app are for personal tracking and general informational purposes only and do not constitute medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional medical advice.

If you have a medical concern, unusual bleeding, severe pain, significant menstrual irregularities, or any condition requiring medical judgment, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.

9. Children's Privacy

The app is not specifically designed as a child-directed service. We do not intentionally create child user accounts or collect children's personal information through the app. If you create a local record for your daughter or another family member, that record is handled as local data on the device in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features, data practices, applicable laws, or platform policies change. The updated policy will be published on this page, together with an updated "Last Updated" date.
