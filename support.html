常见问题（FAQ）

Q1：为什么在 iPhone 上打开 App 是空白的？
A：本开发者的部分产品为独立 Apple Watch 应用，iPhone 仅作为安装容器，不包含游戏内容。请在 Apple Watch 上找到对应 App 图标并启动。
Q2：手表上找不到 App 怎么办？
A：请在 iPhone 的「Watch」应用中，向下滑动找到已安装的应用列表，确认该 App 已开启「在 Apple Watch 上显示 App」。
Q3：心率提醒是医疗功能吗？
A：不是。心率数据仅用于游戏内玩法与休闲提醒，所有提示仅供娱乐参考，不构成医疗诊断或治疗建议。如有健康疑虑，请咨询专业医疗机构。
Q4：应用是单机游戏吗？会收集我的数据吗？
A：本开发者发布的应用均为单机休闲游戏，不强制联网，不主动收集用户个人信息。
Q5：如何申请退款？
A：App Store 购买的退款需通过苹果官方渠道处理，请访问 Apple 支持页面或联系 Apple 客服申请。

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: Why is the app blank when opened on iPhone?
A: Some products by this developer are standalone Apple Watch apps. The iPhone app only serves as an installation container and does not include gameplay content. Please find the app icon on your Apple Watch and launch it there.
Q2: I cannot find the app on my Apple Watch. What should I do?
A: Open the "Watch" app on your iPhone, scroll down to the list of installed apps, and make sure "Show App on Apple Watch" is enabled for the app.
Q3: Is the heart-rate reminder a medical feature?
A: No. Heart-rate data is used solely for in-game mechanics and casual reminders. All prompts are for entertainment reference only and do not constitute medical diagnosis or treatment advice. If you have health concerns, please consult a professional medical institution.
Q4: Are these offline games? Will my data be collected?
A: All apps published by this developer are standalone casual games, do not require a mandatory internet connection, and do not actively collect personal user data.
Q5: How do I request a refund?
A: Refunds for App Store purchases must be processed through Apple's official channels. Please visit the Apple Support page or contact Apple Customer Support to request a refund.
