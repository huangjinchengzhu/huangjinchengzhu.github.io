一、概述

本应用「二吃一」(英文名 Two Beat One)为休闲益智棋类应用。本应用尊重每一位用户的个人隐私。本隐私政策用于向您说明本应用收集、使用、存储用户信息的相关规则。使用本应用即代表您同意本隐私政策全部条款。

二、信息收集说明

  1. 本应用不会收集您的个人身份信息、位置信息、通讯录、相册、设备标识符等敏感个人信息。
  2. 本应用提供两种对战方式:单机对战(人机对战、双人同机)完全无需联网即可运行;联网对战(Wi-Fi 对战)仅在您主动发起时,通过本地网络在设备间直接连接。对战过程只传输走棋坐标等对局必需数据,不经过任何服务器,不会上传到互联网。
  3. 应用进度、战绩与设置仅保存在您的设备本地,仅用于应用本身功能,不会向外传输。

三、权限说明

Wi-Fi 对战需要两台设备连接同一 Wi-Fi 网络(或热点)。系统可能弹出"本地网络"权限询问,该权限仅用于在您主动发起对战时发现并连接附近设备,本应用绝不在后台使用网络扫描,也不读取您网络中的任何其他数据。

四、第三方服务

本应用不含第三方 SDK,不会向任何第三方共享、转让您的任何用户数据。

五、儿童用户

本应用面向全年龄段用户。如为未成年人,请在监护人阅读并同意本隐私政策后使用本软件。

六、您的权利

因为本应用不收集您的个人信息,无个人信息可提供查阅、删除。如您有任何隐私相关疑问,可以通过邮箱 280880560@qq.com 联系开发者。

Privacy Policy for Two Beat One

Last updated: September 03, 2026

Two Beat One is a casual board APP, We attach great importance to your privacy. This application does NOT collect, store, transmit or share any of your personal information.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require you to register any user account.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, photos or any personally identifiable information.
  • We do not track your usage behavior, app records or device-identifiable data.
  • We do not integrate third-party advertising SDK, third-party analytics or tracking tools.

2. App Modes and Networking

  • Offline modes (Play vs AI and pass-and-play) run entirely offline — no network access is required.
  • Wi-Fi Battle connects two devices directly over your local network only when you start a battle. Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi network (or hotspot). Only move coordinates needed for the app are transmitted directly between the two devices.
  • Battles go through no server and no internet; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

3. Local Network Permission

The system may ask for "Local Network" permission when you start a Wi-Fi Battle. This permission is used only to discover and connect to the other device during a battle you initiate. The app never scans networks in the background and never reads any other data on your network.

4. Local Data Storage

All app-related progress data (records, win rate, difficulty) is stored locally on your device only. App data never leaves your device and is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

5. Third-Party Services

This App does not use any third-party SDK and does not share any data with any third party.

6. Children

This App is suitable for all ages. If you are under the age of majority, please review this policy with your parent or guardian before using the app.

7. Your Rights

Because we do not collect personal information, there is no personal data to access or delete. If you have any privacy concerns, you can reach the developer at: 280880560@qq.com

8. Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be shown on this page, and the update takes effect when the page is updated.
