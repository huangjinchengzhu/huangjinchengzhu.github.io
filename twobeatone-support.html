二吃一：民间四子棋 — 技术支持

这是一款独立 Apple Watch 传统民间棋类游戏。无账号登录，无广告，无内购。

游戏简介

流传于田间地头的古老乡土棋戏，4×4棋盘。支持单机AI对局，也支持两台Apple Watch蓝牙双人对战。三十秒快速上手，棋理变化丰富。

完整游戏规则

  • 棋盘：4×4共16个交叉点，双方各4枚棋子。
  • 轮流走棋，每次移动一枚棋子，移动到相邻交叉点。
  • 二吃一：同一条直线（横、竖、对角线），己方两颗棋子夹住对方一颗棋子，中间无空隙，即可吃掉对方这枚棋子。
  • 当一方仅剩1枚棋子，则该方失败，对局结束。

常见问题

  • Q：蓝牙双人对战怎么使用？
    A：两台Apple Watch靠近，开启双人模式，等待配对完成即可对弈。
  • Q：游戏闪退怎么办？
    A：重启手表，重启App；更新手表系统到最新版本。
  • Q：可以保存棋局吗？
    A：当前版本不支持存档。

Two Beat One: Folk Four‑Piece Chess — Technical Support

An independent traditional folk board game for Apple Watch. No account required, no ads, no in‑app purchases.

Game Introduction

An ancient folk board game passed down among local communities. 4×4 game board. Play against AI in solo mode, or Bluetooth two‑player matches between two Apple Watches. Easy to learn in 30 seconds with deep tactical possibilities.

Game Rules

  • Board: 4×4 grid with 16 intersections. Each player has four pieces.
  • Players take turns moving one piece to an adjacent intersection.
  • Capture Rule: Sandwich an opponent’s single piece between two of your pieces on straight line (horizontal, vertical, diagonal) with no gaps, to capture that enemy piece.
  • The player reduced to only one piece loses the game.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Q: How to use Bluetooth multiplayer?
    A: Bring two Apple Watches close, enable multiplayer mode and wait for pairing.
  • Q: App crashes?
    A: Restart your Apple Watch and app, update watchOS to latest version.
  • Q: Can I save game progress?
    A: Saving game states is not supported in current version.
