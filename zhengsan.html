二、应用功能与运行方式

争三（Morris Nine）按照单机游戏模式设计， 游戏核心功能在用户设备本地运行。 应用不要求用户注册账号，也不要求用户登录开发者运营的服务器。

根据当前版本的单机设计，游戏进度、对局状态、分数及相关设置 （如应用实际提供这些功能）可在设备本地进行处理。 开发者不会通过游戏主动将这些本地游戏记录上传到开发者运营的服务器。

三、应用处理的信息

为实现游戏运行及相关功能，应用可能在设备本地处理以下信息， 具体以当前版本实际提供的功能为准：

  • 本地游戏进度及对局状态；
  • 游戏得分、游戏结果或历史记录（如有）；
  • 用户自行选择的游戏设置及偏好；
  • 应用正常运行所需的本地状态信息。

上述信息如由应用产生或保存，将用于运行游戏、保留本地进度、 展示游戏结果或提供设置功能。 根据当前单机设计，这些本地游戏数据不会由开发者主动上传至开发者服务器。

本应用不要求用户主动提交姓名、电话号码、家庭住址、 身份证件信息或账号密码来使用核心游戏功能。

重要说明： 本政策必须与实际发布的应用版本一致。 如果应用集成广告、统计分析、崩溃报告或其他第三方 SDK， 这些组件可能按照其功能处理设备信息或应用使用数据。 开发者应核实实际集成组件的数据处理行为， 并在本政策及 Google Play 数据安全表单中如实说明。

四、数据收集与第三方共享

根据当前单机版本的设计，开发者不会主动将本地游戏记录上传到自己的服务器， 也不会出售这些本地游戏记录。

如果当前版本未集成会收集或传输用户数据的第三方服务， 则应用不会通过第三方服务进行此类数据收集或共享。

如果应用实际集成广告服务、统计分析工具、崩溃报告工具、 在线功能或其他第三方 SDK， 则相关服务可能按照其自身的数据处理规则处理信息。 开发者将根据实际情况更新本政策及 Google Play 数据安全申报。

五、数据存储、保留与删除

游戏运行所需的本地进度、对局状态或设置（如有） 存储在用户自己的设备上。 开发者不会在自己运营的服务器上保留这些本地游戏记录。

用户可以使用游戏实际提供的删除功能删除相关记录。 如果应用没有提供单独的删除入口， 用户可以通过 Android 系统设置清除应用数据， 或卸载应用。

清除应用数据或卸载应用可能导致保存在设备上的游戏进度、 游戏记录及设置被删除。 在执行删除操作前，建议用户确认是否需要保留相关数据。

如果未来版本增加用户账号、云存档或服务器数据存储功能， 开发者将相应更新本政策，并说明相关数据的保留和删除方式。

六、数据安全

用户应妥善保管设备，并使用适当的设备锁屏和系统安全设置。 由于任何电子存储方式都无法保证绝对安全， 我们建议用户采取合理措施保护自己的设备。

七、应用权限

应用应仅请求实现其实际功能所必需的 Android 系统权限。 如果某个版本需要访问设备上的文件、照片、麦克风、位置 或其他受保护资源，开发者应说明相关权限的使用目的， 并确保权限使用符合应用实际功能及 Google Play 政策。

八、儿童隐私

本应用的目标受众以 Google Play 商品详情页和 Play Console 中的实际设置为准。

2. App Functionality and Operation

Morris Nine is designed as a standalone, single-player game. Its core functionality runs locally on the user's device. The app does not require users to register an account or sign in to a developer-operated server.

Under the current standalone design, game progress, match state, scores, and related settings, where supported by the app, may be processed locally. The developer does not actively upload these local game records to a developer-operated server.

3. Information Processed by the App

To operate the game and provide its features, the app may process the following information locally, depending on the features actually included in the current version:

  • Local game progress and match state;
  • Scores, game results, or gameplay history, if available;
  • Game settings and preferences selected by the user;
  • Local state information required for the app to function.

If generated or stored by the app, this information is used to run the game, retain local progress, display results, or provide settings. Under the current standalone design, the developer does not actively upload this local game data to a developer-operated server.

The core game does not require users to submit their names, phone numbers, home addresses, identity documents, or account passwords.

Important: This policy must match the app version actually released. If the app integrates advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or other third-party SDKs, those components may process device or app-usage information. The developer must verify the actual data practices of all integrated components and accurately disclose them in this policy and the Google Play Data safety form.

4. Data Collection and Third-Party Sharing

Under the current standalone design, the developer does not actively upload local game records to its own servers or sell those local records.

If the released version does not include third-party services that collect or transmit user data, the app does not use such services for that purpose.

If the app integrates advertising, analytics, crash reporting, online features, or other third-party SDKs, those services may process information according to their own data practices. The developer will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosure as appropriate.

5. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Local progress, match state, or settings required for gameplay, if any, are stored on the user's device. The developer does not retain these local game records on its own servers.

Users may delete relevant records using any deletion feature provided by the game. If the app does not provide a separate deletion option, users can clear the app's data through Android system settings or uninstall the app.

Clearing app data or uninstalling the app may delete locally stored game progress, records, and settings. Users should confirm whether they need to retain any data before deleting it.

If a future version introduces user accounts, cloud saves, or server-side data storage, this policy will be updated to explain the applicable data retention and deletion procedures.

6. Data Security

The app is designed for standalone use and primarily relies on the user's device and Android security features to protect local data.

Users should keep their devices secure and use appropriate screen locks and system security settings. No electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. App Permissions

The app should request only Android permissions necessary for its actual functionality. If a version requires access to files, photos, the microphone, location, or other protected resources, the purpose should be explained and permission use must comply with the app's functionality and Google Play policies.
